**Oracle E-Business Suite** SQL report from the [Enginatics Library](https://www.enginatics.com/reports/ozf-offers/?utm_source=oraclereporting.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=oracle-ebs-sql&utm_content=ozf-offers) powered by [Blitz Report™](https://www.enginatics.com/blitz-report/?utm_source=oraclereporting.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=oracle-ebs-sql&utm_content=ozf-offers).
**Oracle EBS module:** [Trade Management](/modules/trade-management/ "Oracle EBS Trade Management reports") · [all Trade Management reports](/modules/trade-management/)  

## Overview
Trade Management offers with their type, status, dates, owner, market eligibility and budget source, and the amounts of the offer checkbook in the offer currency: committed, utilized, earned, paid and unpaid.

Committed is the approved budget requests less the transfers back to the budget. Utilized, Earned and Paid follow the rules of OZF Budget Utilization. The report lists the offers that the Advanced Pricing operating unit security allows: global offers and the offers of the operating units the responsibility can access.
